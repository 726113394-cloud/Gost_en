🎯 Core Gameplay
📖 Expand for details

Game Flow

  1. Preparation Phase — Players enter the matchmaking queue; the game starts once full
  2. Infection Phase — Ghosts chase and infect Humans, who can pick up items to defend themselves
  3. Divine Guardian Phase — Automatically activates when the remaining Humans ≤ the trigger count
  4. Demon Hunter Phase — Starts at 90 seconds remaining; Humans with Divine Guardian become Demon Hunters and fight back

Two Factions

FactionDescription
👤 HumanStarting identity; becomes a Ghost once infected
👻 GhostInfects Humans to make them join the Ghost faction; the Mother is the Ghost leader

Special Roles

RoleDescription
🛡️ Divine GuardianBlocks 3 Ghost attacks; each attack randomly teleports the attacker
🎯 Demon HunterConverted from a Human during the Demon Hunter phase; wields the Reaper to fight back
👑 MotherGhost faction leader; can attack Demon Hunters and infect normal Humans
✨ RedeemerPermanent role, up to 2 per match; holds the Holy Redemption item to convert Ghosts back to Humans

⚔️ Demon Hunter Phase
📖 Expand for details
📅 v2.2.2 ~ v2.3.0
📅 v2.3.1+ Current

⚔️ Demon Hunter Phase v2.2.2 ~ v2.3.0

  • All Humans with Divine Guardian become Demon Hunters
  • Demon Hunters hold the Reaper item and kill Ghosts based on damage dealt (configurable)
  • Normal Ghosts cannot infect Humans and can only evade the hunt
  • The Mother can attack Demon Hunters, dealing a configurable amount of damage each hit
  • The Mother must hit 3 times to break a Demon Hunter's Divine Guardian
  • All Holy Redemption items are removed
  • Ghost health is adjusted (Normal Ghost 2❤, Mother Ghost 3❤)
  • Healing is disabled during the Demon Hunter phase (natural regen, hunger, and potions all ineffective)
  • Killed Ghosts enter spectator mode (respawn time configurable)

⚔️ Demon Hunter Phase (Reworked) v2.3.1+ Current

🗡️ Reaper Weapon Rework

  • Left-click single target: Normal Ghost killed in 2 hits, Mother Ghost in 4 hits (pure hit-count based, independent of health)
  • Right-click AoE skill "Harvest": 4-block radius AoE, 10s cooldown, impact particles + wither death sound
  • Attack cooldown 2s

👻 Ghost Revival Mechanic (Improved)

  • After being killed, enters a death state: invisible but free to move (no position lock, no slowdown, no flight)
  • 10-second revival countdown (shown on the ActionBar)
  • On revival, randomly teleported to a coordinate inside the game area (not in place)
  • Restores the original identity (Normal Ghost / Mother) and rejoins the match
  • A red-yellow BURST firework + explosion particles + wither death sound bloom at the kill location
  • Kill subtitle (bilingual): 💥 Elimination! / 💥 击杀成功!

👑 Mother Evolution System (New in v2.3.1)

  • Trigger: Demon Hunter phase with more than 5 players in the match
  • Spawn: A glowing emerald is placed at a random coordinate in the game area (enchant particles + glow marker)
  • Pickup requirement: Only Normal Ghosts can pick it up
  • Effect: Transforms into the Mother Ghost, gains Mother health, and can hunt Demon Hunters
  • Announcement / subtitle / sound (bilingual): ✦ A Mother Upgrade Emerald has appeared! / ✦ 游戏区域出现母体升级绿宝石！
    Transform subtitle: 👑 You are the Mother Ghost! / 👑 你成为了母体鬼!
    Broadcast: [Player] picked up the Mother Emerald and transformed into the Mother Ghost!
    Sounds: challenge complete cue + ender dragon growl
  • The emerald is automatically cleaned up when the game ends

⚔️ v2.3.3 Update — Damage-based Reaper & Spectator Respawn

  • Reaper (damage-based): left-click deals the configured damage and shows damage + remaining HP; at 0 HP the Ghost enters spectator mode with a respawn countdown
  • Harvest: right-click 4-block AoE, same damage, shockwave particles + wither death sound; 10s cooldown
  • Ghost death → Spectator → Respawn: death puts the Ghost into Spectator Mode (no more invisibility compromise), showing an ActionBar countdown, then switching back to Survival Mode at a random point in the game area
  • Demon Hunter killed by the Mother: immediately becomes a normal Ghost and continues after the respawn countdown
  • Mother Evolution Emerald: now spawns during the Holy Guardian phase (was Demon Hunter phase); location configurable as fixed / area center / random

🛡️ Demon Hunter Phase Defense Rules

  • Faction damage protection: Demon Hunters cannot damage Humans/Demon Hunters; Ghosts cannot damage Ghosts (the Mother cannot kill Normal Ghosts); Humans cannot damage Humans
  • Death interception: Real death events for Ghosts/Demon Hunters are cancelled — no respawn point teleport
  • Environmental damage immunity: Ghosts/Demon Hunters are immune to fall/lava/fire and other environmental damage
  • Normal healing: Healing is no longer disabled; players can recover normally

👑 Mother Attacking Demon Hunters

  • Demon Hunter without Divine Guardian: Mother lands 4 hits → infection (enters spectator mode)
  • Demon Hunter with Divine Guardian: First 3 hits to break the guardian, then 4 hits to infect

🚫 Mother Root

  • Before the match starts, the Mother is randomly locked to a coordinate in the game area and cannot move for 20 seconds
🧰 Item System
📖 View item descriptions

Items are distributed on a timer during the match — the interval is configurable (default: every 60 seconds) — and items also spawn randomly in the area. When items are distributed, all player positions are revealed for 5 seconds.

Inventory management (v2.3.1): Up to 9 items during a match. Items cannot be placed in the inventory, do not stack, and duplicates are not allowed (except universal items). The Reaper / Holy Redemption are forced into the first slot.

🗡️ Rush Spear (v2.3.2 update):
• Right-click to dash forward 5 blocks; the item is consumed immediately.
• All-version compatible: displays as a Trident on <1.21.4 and as a Spear on ≥1.21.4 — identical functionality.
• Sound: spear dash sound (built into the game).

🍖 Hunger System
📖 Expand for details

New in v2.3.2 — In-match hunger drain, configurable and toggleable. In-match hunger is independent of the world outside; after settlement the pre-match food level is restored.

⚙️ Core Mechanics

  • All players' food level is restored to full when entering a match
  • Sprinting during the game drains food normally
  • In-match hunger is independent of outside hunger; the pre-match state is restored after settlement
  • This feature supports a toggle and restore speed configuration

👤 Human Crouch Recovery

  • Press [Shift] to crouch to restore 1 hunger per second

👻 Ghost Crouch Recovery

  • Press [Shift] to crouch to restore 1 hunger every 0.8 seconds

🔄 Faction Conversion

  • Food level is restored to full when switching factions

⚔️ Demon Hunter Phase

  • Food level is restored to full and no longer drains during the Demon Hunter phase

Configuration options are located under the food node in config.yml, supporting toggles, restore speed and other parameters.

👻 Ghost-to-Human Conversion
📖 Expand for details

When 3 minutes remain in the game, the system randomly converts some non-Mother Ghost players back to the Human faction (disabled by default; can be enabled in the config).

  • Converted players keep their accumulated time record as a Ghost
  • They receive a 20% bonus compensation
  • They also inherit the prize earned while playing as a Ghost
🏆 Economy & Rewards
📖 Expand for details

Entry Fee & Prize Pool

Players must pay an entry fee to join (default 100 coins); the fee flows into the Human prize pool. Servers can also set an extra bonus (default 5000 coins) to increase the total pool.

Victory Prize Distribution

Winning SideDistribution Rule
👤 Human VictoryHumans receive 70% of the pool, Ghosts receive 30%
👻 Ghost VictoryGhosts receive 100% of the pool

Individual Prize Calculation

FactionDistribution Method
👤 Human100% distributed by survival time ratio
👻 Ghost70% by Ghost survival time + 30% by infection count ratio

Demon Hunter Rewards

Each Ghost killed by a Demon Hunter grants 30% of the Human prize pool; the Mother killing a Demon Hunter grants a 50% extra reward (higher than a normal infection reward).

Prize Leaderboard

A prize leaderboard is shown after the game, with 🥇🥈🥉 medals for the top three.

✨ Special Systems
📖 Expand for details

🌑 Darkness + Sprint Coexist

Uses the native Minecraft DARKNESS effect (1.19+) or BLINDNESS (older versions).
An attribute modifier adds 30% movement speed to offset the darkness effect's impact on sprinting.
Player sprint state is checked every 10 ticks to automatically restore blocked sprinting.
Admins and creative-mode players are also affected by the darkness effect.

👻 Ghost Player Particles

Ghost players display continuous orbiting particle effects.
Mother Ghost: red particles; Normal Ghost: green particles.
Supports 21 particle types with customizable RGB colors.
Whether to display during the preparation phase is configurable.

💬 Multi-Language System (v2.3.3 · six language files)

Supports Chinese / English / Japanese / Spanish / French / German.
Language files live in plugins/Gost/lang/<locale>.yml and can be edited freely.
Select the language with config.yml → language.default or /gostadmin lang.
Changes apply on /gost reload; smart fallback and error isolation are kept.

❤️ Heartbeat System

Humans hear a looping Warden heartbeat sound during the game.
Admins can toggle it via /gostadmin heartbeat.

🎒 Inventory Management System (v2.3.1)

Up to 9 items during a match.
Items cannot be placed in the inventory (forced back to the hotbar).
Items do not stack, duplicates are not allowed, and two Adrenalines are not allowed (except universal items).
Reaper / Holy Redemption are forced into the first slot.

🔥 Selection Particle Frame (v2.3.1)

A flame particle frame is automatically displayed after setting a selection, lasting 30 seconds.
Helps admins visually confirm the game area boundary.

🌐 Bilingual Subtitles (v2.3.1)

Core events such as revival waiting, respawn, harvest, redemption and Mother root now have bilingual (Chinese/English) subtitles.
Automatically switched based on the server language setting.

📦 Version & Server Compatibility

Supports 1.20.x – 1.21.x.
Bukkit / Spigot / Paper / Purpur and other Bukkit-family servers are fully supported; Folia is scheduler-compatible.
Sponge and proxies (BungeeCord / Velocity / Waterfall) are not applicable.
Particles / sounds / attributes / enchantments are resolved at runtime across versions (ApiCompat / ParticleCompat / SoundCompat).
